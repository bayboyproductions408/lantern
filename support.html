The Bible, read aloud

Open the app, press play, and it reads scripture aloud from wherever you left off — chapter after chapter, with no account and no charge.

Getting started

  • Press the large play button to begin listening from your saved place.
  • Use the Library tab to jump to any book and chapter.
  • The app remembers your position automatically, even after you close it.

Common questions

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Every translation is stored inside the app, so reading and listening work fully offline.

How do I change the voice or reading speed?

Open the Settings tab. You can choose the voice offered by your device, adjust the speed, and pick a tone — Soothing is the default and is the slowest and gentlest.

How do I switch to Spanish?

In Settings, change the translation to Reina-Valera. The reading voice switches to Spanish automatically, and your place in the text is kept.

Which translations are included?

King James Version, Bible in Basic English, and Reina-Valera (1909). All three are in the public domain.

Can I set it to stop after a while?

Yes. The sleep timer can stop playback after a set number of minutes, or at the end of the current chapter.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. There is no subscription, no paywall, and no locked content. We do not want anyone to have to pay to read or hear the Bible.
